# Quiz scoring rubric (integers 0-4)

Score each theory on five axes, from the theory's OWN position as described in its brief (not your view, not the family average). Use the whole range; 2 is a real middle position, not "unknown". If a theory truly takes no position on an axis, use null.

mm  Matter vs mind: what is ultimately real?
  0 Only physical matter; mind is physical processes (reductive materialism, eliminativism, illusionism)
  1 Physical world only, but mind is a real higher-level or non-reducible physical property (non-reductive physicalism, biological naturalism, emergentism within physics)
  2 Neither is basic: mind and matter are two aspects of one underlying reality, or experience is a basic property of matter (neutral monism, dual-aspect, Russellian monism, panpsychism)
  3 Mind and matter are both real and distinct (substance or property dualism, soul theories)
  4 Mind/consciousness is primary; matter is derived from it or appears within it (idealism, non-duality, cosmic consciousness)

fu  How basic is consciousness?
  0 It is not what it seems: an illusion, a user-illusion or a confused concept (eliminativism, illusionism)
  1 A late, rare product of complex brains or advanced cognition (e.g. needs higher-order thought, language, a global workspace)
  2 Arises across living systems, graded with biological complexity (life, homeostasis, affect, basic animal minds)
  3 Present well beyond brains, in simple systems or in matter (IIT-style, panpsychism, panprotopsychism)
  4 The ground of reality itself; everything else depends on it (idealism, cosmic mind, God/Brahman as consciousness)

lo  Where is consciousness?
  0 Specific brain regions or mechanisms
  1 The whole brain (or whole nervous system) as a working system
  2 Brain, body and environment together (embodied, enactive, extended, relational, social)
  3 Spread across nature or many systems, not tied to individual organisms (field theories, panpsychist distribution)
  4 Non-local, cosmic or transcendent; not located in space (universal mind, soul realms, beyond physics)

ev  What kind of evidence does the theory mainly rest on?
  0 Empirical neuroscience or experiment; makes testable predictions
  1 Science plus philosophical argument, roughly equally
  2 Mainly philosophical analysis and argument
  3 Philosophy together with contemplative, spiritual or first-person practice
  4 Mainly religious tradition, revelation, mystical or anomalous experience (NDEs, psi, psychedelic reports)

qu  Does it need quantum physics?
  0 No role for quantum effects; classical physics/biology suffices or physics isn't the issue
  2 Quantum physics plays a possible or supporting role
  4 Quantum physics (or new physics beyond it) is essential to how consciousness works
  (use 0, 2 or 4 only)
